# Demo Plan

## Five-minute portfolio demo

### 1. Problem — 30 seconds
Show a token with rising volume and explain that price alone does not reveal whether activity is fundamental, leveraged, social, or liquidity-driven.

### 2. Run scan — 60 seconds
Execute an offline fixture or live read-only scan.

### 3. Inspect anomaly — 60 seconds
Show:
- current volume;
- 7/30-day baseline;
- volume/market-cap;
- price confirmation;
- trigger reason.

### 4. Research/evidence — 60 seconds
Open the evidence bundle and show primary vs secondary sources, timestamps, and catalyst classification.

### 5. HMM report — 60 seconds
Show independent Hype, Metrics, Mechanics, Trading, and Investment scores.

### 6. Safety architecture — 30 seconds
Demonstrate Bankr launch simulation and show that no transaction is broadcast.

## Interview talking point
The most important product behavior is not finding a catalyst. It is being able to say **“none found”** without manufacturing one.
